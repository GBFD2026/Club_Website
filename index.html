About Our Club

We are a friendly folk dancing club. We meet every Monday evening* at Grundisburgh Village Hall from 7:30 pm to 9:00 pm. The charge for the evening is just £2.00 (and zero for people coming along to their first evening!).

All dances are called. We dance a wide range of styles, eg, ceilidh or barn dance-type, Playford, Apted, and a lot of dances composed much more recently, many in these traditional styles, by, eg, Ron Coxall, Gary Roodman, Fried de Metz Herman, Joseph Pimentel and Charles Bolton. The emphasis is on getting through the dances and having fun! Most of the dances are fairly simple but we usually have one or two dances during the evening which are a bit more challenging!

Live Music Evenings: From time to time we have live music (this year it has been roughly every 6 weeks) – the next such occasion will be Monday, 14 December, 2026.

*Except on Public Holidays, the Christmas festive period and the second half of August.
